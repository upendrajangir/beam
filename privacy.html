Beam Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

Beam is published by Coder's Pantry. This policy covers the Beam app for Meta Quest and the Beam app for Android phones.

What Beam collects

Nothing. Beam has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not send any data to Coder's Pantry or to any third party.

How Beam uses your files

Beam moves files only between devices on your own local network. The phone app shares the one folder you choose. The headset app reads files from that folder, or from an SMB share you configure, and saves them to the headset's storage. Files never pass through the internet or any server outside your network.

What stays on your devices

Beam stores its settings on each device: the address of the phone or share, the password you set, the chosen folder and the number of connections. It also keeps small progress records so a paused copy can resume. These never leave the device and are removed when you uninstall Beam.

Permissions

  • Network and Wi-Fi access: to find the phone on your local network and copy files from it.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices: for the optional Wi-Fi Direct link. Beam does not use it to determine your location.
  • All files access (phone only): to share the folder you choose.
  • Notifications (phone only): to show that sharing is on.

Security

The phone app accepts connections only from devices on the same local network, and only with the password you set. Repeated wrong passwords are slowed and then blocked.

Children

Beam does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the Beam issue tracker.
