Beam

Beam copies files from your phone to your Meta Quest over your own Wi-Fi. No cable, no cloud upload, no account.

How it works

  1. Install Beam on your Android phone and choose a folder to share.
  2. Open Beam on your Quest. It finds your phone on the local network.
  3. Pick a file and press Copy.

Each file is read over many connections at once, so a copy runs as fast as the Wi-Fi link allows. Copies can be paused, resumed and queued. Beam also reads from SMB shares and can link the phone and headset over Wi-Fi Direct.
